# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales revenue, total orders, average order value, product performance, and country-wise revenue.

## Objectives
- Analyze total sales revenue and order performance.
- Identify the top 10 selling products.
- Compare revenue across different countries.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Provide business insights and recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Kaggle Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1: Sales Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Quantity
- Monthly Revenue Trend

### Page 2: Product & Country Analysis
- Top 10 Selling Products
- Revenue by Country
- Key Business Insights and Recommendations

## Key Insights
- The United Kingdom contributes the highest revenue in the dashboard.
- The top-selling products can be identified by sales quantity.
- Monthly revenue trends help understand changes in sales performance.
- Businesses can focus on popular products and explore opportunities in lower-revenue countries.
